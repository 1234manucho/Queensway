What ages can join Queensway?

Please contact the school to confirm its current age eligibility and entry points. We will help you explore the appropriate stage for your child.

Where can I find the fee structure?

Request the current fee structure directly from the school office. Fees, payment arrangements and any additional costs should be confirmed before enrolment.

Does an enquiry reserve a place?

No. An enquiry starts a conversation. A place is only confirmed after the school has completed its admissions process and communicated its decision.

What should I bring on a visit?

Bring your questions and let the school know about any access requirements. The team will confirm the visit arrangements and advise if anything else is needed.

Can I discuss my child’s individual needs?

Yes. Use the enquiry form to request a conversation. Please avoid sharing sensitive records through the initial form; discuss an appropriate channel with the school.
