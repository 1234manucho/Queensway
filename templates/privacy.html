Care in every conversation

Your privacy matters.

How this website handles the information you choose to share.

Information you provide

Enquiry forms ask for a parent or guardian’s name, email address and phone number, together with the information needed to respond. Admissions enquiries may include an optional child’s first name, age range and area of interest. Visit requests can include a preferred date.

How enquiries are used

Submitted information is stored in the school website’s database for authorised staff to review and respond to admissions, contact and visit enquiries. A submission does not confirm a place or a visit. Staff may add internal notes and update the enquiry’s status.

Please share only what is needed

Do not use these forms to submit identity documents, medical records, financial details or other sensitive information. If further information is needed, discuss a suitable channel directly with the school. A parent or guardian should complete enquiries relating to a child.

Cookies and security

This website uses essential session cookies for secure forms, confirmation messages and staff sign-in. The supplied application does not include advertising trackers or third-party analytics. Access to enquiry records requires a staff account.

Contact about your information

To ask about an enquiry or request a correction or deletion, contact the school through the contact page. The school should verify the request before sharing or changing personal information.

Photographs

This website features photographs supplied for Queensway Early Years, showing school life, outdoor activities and the campus. For a question about a photograph or a request to remove one, please contact the school.
